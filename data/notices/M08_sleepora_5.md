# Sleepora 5

> FICTIONAL medicine invented for a course project. Not real medical information.

**Active ingredient:** sleeporazine · **Form:** tablet 5 mg, box of 10 · **Prescription:** required

## What it looks like
Purple round tablet, engraved "SL5". Sold in a box of 10 tablets.

## What it is used for
Short-term treatment of severe insomnia, meaning serious trouble sleeping. It is a controlled medicine available only with a prescription.

## Dosage
Adults: 1 tablet at bedtime, for a maximum of 7 days. Do not take more than 1 tablet per night.

## Warnings
Controlled medicine that can cause dependence. Do not drive after taking it. Do not drink alcohol. Not for people under 18 years or during pregnancy.

## Side effects
Common: morning drowsiness and dizziness. Rare: memory problems and confusion.
