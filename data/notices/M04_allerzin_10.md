# Allerzin 10

> FICTIONAL medicine invented for a course project. Not real medical information.

**Active ingredient:** allerzinine · **Form:** tablet 10 mg, box of 20 · **Prescription:** not required

## What it looks like
Small blue oval tablet with a line across the middle. Sold in a box of 20 tablets.

## What it is used for
Relieves hay fever and allergy symptoms: sneezing, runny nose, itchy eyes and itchy skin.

## Dosage
Adults and children over 12 years: 1 tablet once a day, in the evening. Do not take more than 1 tablet per day.

## Warnings
May cause drowsiness, so do not drive or use machines after taking it. Do not drink alcohol. Do not use if you have severe kidney disease.

## Side effects
Common: drowsiness and dry mouth. Rare: headache and fast heartbeat.
