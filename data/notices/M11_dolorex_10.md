# Dolorex 10

> FICTIONAL medicine invented for a course project. Not real medical information.

**Active ingredient:** dolorexone · **Form:** tablet 10 mg, box of 14 · **Prescription:** required

## What it looks like
Orange round tablet, engraved "DX10". Sold in a box of 14 tablets.

## What it is used for
Relieves severe pain that other painkillers cannot control. It is a controlled medicine available only with a prescription.

## Dosage
Adults: 1 tablet every 8 hours when prescribed. Maximum 3 tablets in 24 hours.

## Warnings
Controlled medicine with a risk of dependence. Do not drink alcohol. Do not drive. Do not use if you have breathing problems.

## Side effects
Common: constipation, drowsiness and nausea. Rare: slow breathing, in which case call the emergency services.
