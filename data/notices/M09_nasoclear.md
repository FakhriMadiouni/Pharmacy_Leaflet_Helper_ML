# Nasoclear

> FICTIONAL medicine invented for a course project. Not real medical information.

**Active ingredient:** nasoclearine · **Form:** nasal spray 0.1%, bottle of 15 ml · **Prescription:** not required

## What it looks like
Small white plastic bottle with a spray nozzle and a green label, 15 ml.

## What it is used for
Relieves a blocked nose caused by a cold or an allergy.

## Dosage
Adults and children over 12 years: 1 spray in each nostril, up to 3 times a day. Do not use for more than 5 days in a row.

## Warnings
Using it for more than 5 days can make the blockage worse. Do not use if you have glaucoma. Do not share the bottle.

## Side effects
Common: dry nose and mild stinging. Rare: nosebleed.
