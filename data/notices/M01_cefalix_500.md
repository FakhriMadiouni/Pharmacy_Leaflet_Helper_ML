# Cefalix 500

> FICTIONAL medicine invented for a course project. Not real medical information.

**Active ingredient:** cefalixane · **Form:** tablet 500 mg · **Prescription:** not required

## What it looks like
White round tablet, engraved "CX" on one side. Sold in a blue box of 16 tablets.

## What it is used for
Relieves mild to moderate pain such as headache, toothache and muscle aches. It also lowers fever.

## Dosage
Adults and children over 12 years: 1 tablet every 6 hours if needed, with water. Do not take more than 4 tablets in 24 hours. Do not use for more than 3 days for fever or 5 days for pain without medical advice.

## Warnings
Do not use if you are allergic to cefalixane or have severe liver disease. Do not drink alcohol while taking this medicine. Ask a doctor before use during pregnancy. Do not combine with other medicines containing cefalixane.

## Side effects
Rare: mild nausea and skin rash. Stop treatment and see a doctor if you get a rash or swelling of the face.
