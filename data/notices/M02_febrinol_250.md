# Febrinol 250

> FICTIONAL medicine invented for a course project. Not real medical information.

**Active ingredient:** febrinolide · **Form:** strawberry syrup 250 mg per 5 ml, bottle of 100 ml · **Prescription:** not required

## What it looks like
Pink syrup with a strawberry smell, in a 100 ml bottle with a measuring spoon.

## What it is used for
Lowers fever and relieves mild pain in children from 3 months to 12 years.

## Dosage
The dose depends on the weight of the child: 10 mg per kg of body weight, up to 3 times a day, at least 6 hours apart. Use only the measuring spoon supplied. Do not give for more than 3 days without asking a doctor.

## Warnings
Do not give to babies under 3 months. Do not use if the child is allergic to febrinolide or has kidney disease. Keep out of reach of children.

## Side effects
Occasionally: stomach ache and sleepiness. Seek medical help if the child has trouble breathing.
