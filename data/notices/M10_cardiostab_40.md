# Cardiostab 40

> FICTIONAL medicine invented for a course project. Not real medical information.

**Active ingredient:** cardiostabine · **Form:** tablet 40 mg, box of 28 · **Prescription:** required

## What it looks like
Pink oval tablet with a score line, engraved "CS40". Sold in a box of 28 tablets.

## What it is used for
Treats high blood pressure. It is taken for a long time, on a doctor's prescription.

## Dosage
Adults: 1 tablet once a day at the same time each day. Do not stop the treatment without your doctor's advice.

## Warnings
Prescription only. Do not use during pregnancy. Do not use if you have very low blood pressure.

## Side effects
Common: tiredness and dizziness when standing up. Rare: swollen ankles.
