# Tussimel

> FICTIONAL medicine invented for a course project. Not real medical information.

**Active ingredient:** tussimeline · **Form:** syrup, bottle of 200 ml · **Prescription:** not required

## What it looks like
Amber syrup with a honey flavour, in a brown 200 ml bottle.

## What it is used for
Relieves dry, irritating cough without phlegm.

## Dosage
Adults: 10 ml up to 3 times a day. Children from 6 to 12 years: 5 ml up to 3 times a day. Do not use for more than 7 days.

## Warnings
Do not use for a cough with thick phlegm. Not for children under 6 years. The syrup contains sugar, which matters for people with diabetes.

## Side effects
Rare: nausea and mild drowsiness.
