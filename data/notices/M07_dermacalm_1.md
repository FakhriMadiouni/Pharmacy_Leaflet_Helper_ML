# Dermacalm 1%

> FICTIONAL medicine invented for a course project. Not real medical information.

**Active ingredient:** dermacalmide · **Form:** cream 1%, tube of 30 g · **Prescription:** not required

## What it looks like
White cream in a 30 g aluminium tube with an orange cap.

## What it is used for
Relieves itching and redness caused by eczema and mild skin irritation.

## Dosage
Apply a thin layer to the affected skin once or twice a day. Do not use for more than 7 days in a row. Wash your hands after use.

## Warnings
Do not apply to open wounds, to the eyes, or to the face of children under 6 years. Do not use under a tight bandage unless a doctor advises it.

## Side effects
Occasionally: burning or tingling where the cream is applied. Stop if the skin becomes more red.
