# Ocufresh

> FICTIONAL medicine invented for a course project. Not real medical information.

**Active ingredient:** ocufreshine · **Form:** eye drops 0.5%, bottle of 10 ml · **Prescription:** not required

## What it looks like
Small transparent plastic bottle with a white cap and a blue label, 10 ml.

## What it is used for
Relieves dry, tired or irritated eyes, for example after working on a screen.

## Dosage
1 drop in each eye up to 4 times a day. Remove contact lenses before use and wait 15 minutes before putting them back.

## Warnings
Throw the bottle away 28 days after opening. Do not touch the eye with the tip of the bottle. See a doctor if you have eye pain or your vision changes.

## Side effects
Occasionally: brief stinging and blurred vision for a few minutes after use.
