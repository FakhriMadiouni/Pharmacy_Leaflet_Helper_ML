# Gastrovel 20

> FICTIONAL medicine invented for a course project. Not real medical information.

**Active ingredient:** gastrovelane · **Form:** capsule 20 mg, box of 14 · **Prescription:** not required

## What it looks like
Green and white capsule printed "GV20". Sold in a box of 14 capsules.

## What it is used for
Treats heartburn and acid reflux, which is acid coming up from the stomach.

## Dosage
Adults: 1 capsule once a day, in the morning, 30 minutes before breakfast. Treatment lasts 14 days. Swallow the capsule whole with water.

## Warnings
Do not use if you have difficulty swallowing or black stools. See a doctor if symptoms continue after 14 days. Not recommended during breastfeeding.

## Side effects
Common: headache and mild diarrhoea. Rare: dizziness.
