# Bronchoral 100

> FICTIONAL medicine invented for a course project. Not real medical information.

**Active ingredient:** bronchoralin · **Form:** capsule 100 mg, box of 12 · **Prescription:** required

## What it looks like
Yellow capsule printed "BR100". Sold in a box of 12 capsules.

## What it is used for
Antibiotic used to treat bacterial chest infections such as bronchitis. It is available only with a prescription.

## Dosage
Adults: 1 capsule twice a day for 6 days, as prescribed. Finish the full course even if you feel better.

## Warnings
Prescription only. Do not use if you are allergic to antibiotics of the same family. Not suitable during pregnancy.

## Side effects
Common: nausea and diarrhoea. Rare: allergic skin reaction. Stop treatment and see a doctor if it happens.
